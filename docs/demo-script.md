# Demo script — 3 minutes

Target length **3:00**. The screen recording starts on the product. No slides, no music, no
title card. Voice-over only.

---

## 0:00 — The problem, in one breath (voice-over only, product already on screen)

> "A student spends weeks reading scholarship calls, and still applies to programmes they were
> never eligible for — and finds out only after the deadline. Oppulse AI does that reading, shows
> its evidence, and tells you the one thing to do next."

*Screen: the home workspace, already loaded. No navigation yet.*

---

## 0:15 — The agent has already researched (30s)

*Screen: home. Point at the three columns.*

> "Here is my opportunity agent. I described a goal in my own words; it searched the web,
> retrieved the official calls, and checked each requirement against my confirmed profile."

*Point at the run summary in the agent panel: "9 accessible sources, 7 candidates, 1 unreachable."*

> "It also tells me what it could not reach. That is the part most tools hide."

*Point at the context strip: "Working on: Helios Research Fellowship".*

> "And it always says what it is working on. Same agent, same conversation, whether I am on the
> dashboard or on the assistant page — and it knows which opportunity I mean, so it does not ask
> me to repeat myself."

*Point at the opportunity rail: 92, 78, 85, 74, 81.*

> "Seven candidates, ranked against my profile. The numbers are **profile match**, out of a
> hundred — not a probability of getting in."

---

## 0:45 — Open one finding (30s)

*Click: Helios Research Fellowship → deep analysis.*

> "One click and I have the full analysis: fit, eligibility, funding, deadline, confidence — five
> things that are usually conflated."

*Click the **Eligibility** tab.*

> "Requirement by requirement, with the clause from the call and the reason it applies to me."

*Click **Evidence** → **Open drawer**.*

> "Every claim links to the source it came from, with the page, the retrieval time and the source
> quality. If two sources disagree, I get a conflict panel — not a date somebody averaged."

*Point at the Verdant card: "Deadline conflict".*

---

## 1:15 — The one thing to do next (30s)

*Click: **Application workspace**.*

> "I save the opportunity and it becomes a workspace, not a bookmark: a checklist drawn from the
> call, a task list, and one next best action."

*Point at "Confirm your IELTS score — it is the only thing blocking two checklist items and one
requirement."*

> "Notice the recommendation is about *my* missing fact, not about the programme. That is the
> difference."

---

## 1:45 — The AI proposes, I decide (40s)

*Click: the writing studio.*

> "The writing assistant drafts from my confirmed facts. It never overwrites my document — it
> gives me a proposal, and I review the diff."

*Click: **Review changes**.*

> "Word-level diff. And it flags anything it could not ground in my profile."

*Point at the amber "unsupported claim" panel.*

> "If I accept, it becomes a new version. The old one is still there. Nothing is lost, and nothing
> is fabricated."

*Select one sentence in the document; the selection bar appears.*

> "If I select a passage, it rewrites *that* passage and leaves the rest alone. So the diff is
> small enough to actually check."

---

## 2:25 — It keeps watching (25s)

*Click: **Monitoring & alerts**.*

> "I save the mission. A background worker re-checks the sources on a schedule — and I can see
> the real last check and the real next check. It never claims to be watching continuously."

*Point at the alert: "deadline extended by 7 days" and at the last/next check times.*

> "This one is a controlled test event, clearly labelled. A funding increase of fifteen hundred
> euros, and it tells me exactly what to do about it."

---

## 2:50 — The close (10s)

*Screen: back to the home workspace.*

> "Upload a CV, say what you want, and the agent does the reading, shows its evidence, turns the
> right one into an application plan, and keeps watching. That is Oppulse AI."

---

## Recording checklist

- [ ] Voice-over is clear at recording resolution; the screen text is readable when zoomed to 100%
- [ ] The prototype banner is visible — do not crop it out. Honesty is the point.
- [ ] Console is closed. No debugger, no dev tools on screen.
- [ ] One source is unreachable and one deadline conflicts — both must be visible.
- [ ] The video is **unlisted**, not private, on YouTube or Google Drive.
- [ ] Test the link in a private window before submitting.
